## Qualification and interpretation

**The division-free candidate fails the frozen gate.** Its 64.6% overall score
is below 90%; full accuracy of 2/16 and straight accuracy of 13/16 are below
85%; its gain over atan is negative rather than at least ten points. The
passing audit and zero endpoint errors do not overcome these quality failures.
No native-menu validation, live race or preset promotion was triggered.

Independent exact-decimal checks confirmed that the two supplied rules have
the same target on every fixture. All 24 cross-product labels match the
independent atan2 quantizer; the smallest nominal quantization margin is
0.006912 rad and the smallest absolute decimal threshold-comparison margin
is 1.610918 square metres. Finite threshold constants are equivalent on this
protected bank; behavior at arbitrary exact mathematical ties remains untested.
The score difference therefore concerns responses to two question formulations
on matched labels, rather than a changed target policy on these cases.

The question package changes arithmetic operations, ordered magnitude branches
and explicit sign restoration together. The host supplies only raw coordinate
literals and fixed rule constants, with no evaluated current-query intermediate
or target. These results do not identify which arithmetic the model performed,
or establish a general inability to compare products. Coarse angle answers
also cannot identify exact trigonometric execution or optimal driving.

Retain `damped` as the live baseline, Python as the runtime and the 24 m stadium.
End this algebraic-question comparison. A distinct decision-interface experiment
requires its own frozen protocol, implementation checks and qualification;
it is not a continuation earned by this failed gate.
