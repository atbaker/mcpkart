## Interpretation and fairness

A clean three-lap finish is possible without the supplied action policy:
standard101 finished82.83s versus78.45s for its policy control, a4.38s/5.59%
slowdown. The mirrored goal-only attempt was stopped at41.93s and62/120gates;
its policy control finished79.60s cleanly. Both goal attempts stayed off grass
and used no rescues before their respective finish/stop.

The unfinished goal-only run includes a2.197s API response starting at39.3s,
when the previous left+accelerate input remained held. The next measured
bearing was-72.2108°; one hard-right reply later, the 15m point was behind the
kart and the unchanged guard stopped collection. The kart was STILL ON ROAD.
This was a predeclared harness-domain stop, not an endpoint error, crash,
rescue or demonstrated inability to recover. No second attempt replaced it.
Different latency tails and only two starts prevent separating prompt removal
from the spike's causal contribution; the counterfactual is unmeasured.

Median latencies were similar: goal-only210.3ms, policy213.5ms pooled. Goal-only
selected STRAIGHT4/512times versus242/641for policy, and accelerated on all512
queries. The recorded trajectories show more steering oscillation. These are
descriptive behaviors on different states, not accuracy against the removed
policy or evidence that any individual choice was optimal or wrong.

The user prefers game mechanics, factual measurements and question-local
packaging without action-selection coaching. Keep that direction. A sensible
next separately registered test would let the goal-only driver continue through
behind-preview/missed-gate situations using valid factual geometry, so its own
recovery can be assessed. That would remove a restriction inherited from the
old policy, rather than add steering/braking advice. Do not relax this study's
stops retroactively or claim an unobserved recovery succeeded.

The current results do not isolate category4's independent driving effect:
local facts are present in both arms. They establish one clean goal-only
finish and one retained domain-limited stop in this pilot. OpenAI was not
retested without the policy. No default or candidate promotion follows.
